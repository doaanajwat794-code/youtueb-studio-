### #1 · FACE NOT RECOGNIZED — psychological thriller · ⭐ recommended
**Logline:** When every device in her home suddenly stops recognising Elise's face, she
discovers her AR contact lenses have been showing her a face that stopped being hers
decades ago.
- **Hook (0–2 s):** Close-up: a woman's face lit blue by her phone. A red "✕" pulses. Narration: *"My phone doesn't know who I am."*
- **Rule:** Elise, 30s, lives alone in a sleek smart apartment. Everything opens with her face.
- **Crack 1:** The front door refuses her too. **Clue:** a faint blue ring flickers in her iris.
- **Crack 2:** The bathroom mirror goes dark for a second, and the glass shows a grey-haired woman.
- **Push:** A knock. A man in his 50s says gently, *"Mum, the lenses died again."*
- **Twist (≈ 0:47):** Her lenses show her the face she had 30 years ago. The devices see the truth.
- **Echo:** Close-up of her eye as the blue ring flickers back on. She smiles at the phone, and it still says ✕.
- **Cast/locations:** Elise at two ages (locked separately), the son as a voice/silhouette; 2 locations (living room, bathroom/hallway).
- **Why it works:** Instant, relatable hook (face unlock fails), a visual clue, and an emotional twist about identity and technology. Easy to loop.
- **Risk:** Keeping the same woman at two ages consistent. Mitigated by two locked reference sheets.

### #2 · STILL HERE — grief-tech mystery
**Logline:** A father visits his daughter every night, until he realises no one else can
see him, because he is the AI copy of himself that she can't turn off.
- **Hook:** A man stands in rain at a grave. The rain passes *through* his coat. *"I come here every night."*
- **Rule:** Every evening he walks home and tucks his daughter in.
- **Crack 1:** A stranger walks straight through the space he stands in. **Clue:** his umbrella stays dry.
- **Crack 2:** The name on the headstone is his own (said in narration, not rendered as text).
- **Twist:** Pull back: he exists only on her phone screen at the kitchen table. *"Goodnight, Dad."*
- **Echo:** The screen goes dark and reflects her face, echoing the grave shot's reflective puddle.
- **Why it works:** Grief tech is a real, current debate, and the emotion drives shares. **Risk:** melancholy may retain less than suspense; the rain + VFX shots are harder.

### #3 · REGENERATE — meta sci-fi mystery
**Logline:** A man notices his life has started skipping like a badly edited video: then he
sees the cursor.
- **Hook:** A man lifts a coffee cup, and it is full, then empty, then full again, in three hard jump cuts. *"It started with the coffee."*
- **Rule:** An ordinary morning, repeated.
- **Crack 1:** His friend says the same sentence twice with different faces. **Clue:** a tiny progress bar reflected in his window.
- **Crack 2:** His hands flicker between versions.
- **Twist:** Pull out of his world into a vertical phone screen; a thumb hovers over "Regenerate".
- **Echo:** Tap. Frame 1 again, but the coffee is now a different colour. It loops.
- **Why it works:** Very on-brand for an AI channel, a designed loop, and cheap (glitch effects happen in the edit). **Risk:** meta-AI stories can feel gimmicky, and phone-UI text must stay minimal.
