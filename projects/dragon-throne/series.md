# DRAGON THRONE — original dragon roster (v1, 2026-10-05)

Owner moodboard: a House of the Dragon collage (`media/DT/owner_moodboard_hotd.jpg`). Those designs and names are HBO's;
per the originality rule we keep only the *qualities* the owner liked (serpentine red, ancient colossus, golden king,
silver-blue, volcanic) and build ORIGINAL dragons with our own names, colours, horns and silhouettes.
Every dragon has a different SILHOUETTE + COLOUR + BREATH so Veo cannot swap them (lesson from CT E04 C02).

Format: every Short is ONE standalone duel ("FIRE vs ICE — who wins?"), 5 continuous 10 s clips, 30–40 s, no dialogue,
2–3 word on-screen text, clear winner, the next challenger appears in the last second. Pilot: IGNAR vs SYLVRA.
