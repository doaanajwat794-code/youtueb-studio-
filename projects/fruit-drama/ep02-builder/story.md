# FRUIT DRAMA EP02 — "He Left Them for Money" (owner idea 2026-10-12, one standalone video, 6 scenes, ~50 s)
Owner idea: a poor, muscular construction worker leaves his wife and son for a rich woman with a nice car;
she later dumps him for a richer man; he returns regretful, but his wife has a kind new man who cares for her,
his son loves that man, and they do not take him back.
Claude additions: cold-open hook on the ending; the son hides behind the new man (gut punch); a toy he brings back
falls into the rain; PINEAPPLE (known villain from EP01) is the richer man; closing line.

## Cast
- BLUEBERRY (male, new character; not EP01's waitress) — the husband, muscular construction worker (worker clothes; later flashy rich clothes)
- PEACH — his loyal wife, kind, modest
- APRICOT — their little son (about 6)
- STRAWBERRY — the rich woman with the red sports car (owner: people love strawberry; reuse EP01 face, new outfit: loose silk blouse + wide trousers)
- PINEAPPLE — the even richer man she leaves him for (existing reference)
- AVOCADO — a kind, calm man who later cares for PEACH and the boy
