# FRUIT DRAMA — "The Poor Husband Was a Billionaire" (2 parts, 2026-10-08)
Owner brief: a rich fruit gives up his money to find true love; marries; the wife leaves him for a rich fruit;
he returns and takes revenge. Strong drama, never boring, short. Split in two parts so Part 1 ends on a cliffhanger.

## Cast (photoreal anthropomorphic fruit: real fruit head, human body, human clothes)
- MANGO — the hidden billionaire heir, kind, quiet.
- COCONUT — his old, wise, very rich father.
- STRAWBERRY — the beautiful wife who chases money.
- PINEAPPLE — the flashy "rich" rival (his money is borrowed from Mango's family bank).
- BLUEBERRY — a kind waitress; helps Mango in the rain when he has nothing (the real love; pays off in Part 2).
