# THE CROWN TRIAL (series bible, v1, 2026-09-27)

**Format:** YouTube Shorts series, 25–40 s each, 9:16, 1080x1920, English on-screen text, no narration.
Epic, dark medieval fantasy: anthropomorphic animal warriors in ornate armour duel with swords
in a royal tournament. One duel per Short; winners advance until one champion takes the crown.
Style learned from high-performing reels (dark epic fantasy, armoured beasts, 2–3 word hook
text, epic music, fast cuts). All names, characters, kingdom and designs are original.

## Kingdom and event
- **Kingdom:** Aurumvale, a mountain kingdom of black stone and gold.
- **Event:** *The Crown Trial*, held once a generation: whoever wins wears the Crown of Aurumvale.
- **Arena:** a moonlit circular stone coliseum in the snow, rings of torches, black-and-gold
  banners, a raised throne balcony with the empty crown on a velvet stand.

## Bracket (4 episodes)
- **Ep 1 — Semi-final 1:** Kael (lion) vs Rajan (tiger)
- **Ep 2 — Semi-final 2:** Borin (bear) vs Vessa (wolf)
- **Ep 3 — The Final:** winner Ep 1 vs winner Ep 2
- **Ep 4 — The Crown Duel:** the Trial winner vs the masked Reigning Champion (mask falls: black panther)
Winners are decided per episode (option: let comments vote "who wins?" for engagement).

## Episode formula (25–40 s, 10–14 shots of 2–3 s)
1. 0–2 s **Hook:** blades clash in extreme close-up + big text (2–4 words), epic hit.
2. Intro: fighter A title card, fighter B title card (name + title), 1.5 s each.
3. 4–6 exchange shots, ONE action per shot (strike, block, dodge, spin, disarm).
4. Turn: the underdog move.
5. Finish: the winner stands, the loser kneels (no gore, no blood).
6. End card: "NEXT: ___ vs ___" (drives the next episode) + "Who wins? Comment".

## Rules
- Veo does one strong move per 8 s clip; we cut each clip to its best 2–3 s.
- Same armour, colours and weapon for each fighter in every episode (Flow Characters).
- No gore, no blood, no deaths: a duel of honour (brand-safe, Shorts-safe).
- Music: epic original score (Claude) or YouTube Audio Library; no copyrighted soundtracks.
