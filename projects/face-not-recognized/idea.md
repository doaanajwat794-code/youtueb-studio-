# Face Not Recognized: idea (SELECTED by the owner, 2026-09-24)

**One-sentence test:** Elise notices that her phone and door no longer recognize her in a world where
her lenses decide what she sees, and the truth is that she has been seeing her 37-year-old face for
thirty years. The first shot (the faint blue ring in her eye) was the clue.

| Beat | Content |
|---|---|
| Hook (0–2 s) | Face-unlock fails: red light on her face, "Face not recognized" |
| Rule | Her life opens with her face: phone, door |
| Clue | Faint blue ring in her iris; the door estimates age 67; the mirror shows her sweater, ring and eyes on an older woman |
| Twist (≈0:36) | Theo: "Mum… the lenses died again." We see her at 67. |
| Echo | Same close-up as frame 1; the lens glows; the phone welcomes her home |

**Originality check (2026-09-24):** closest known relative is *Sight* (2012, AR contact lenses used
for a dating game), a different premise and twist. Also different from *I'm Not a Robot* (captcha/android).
