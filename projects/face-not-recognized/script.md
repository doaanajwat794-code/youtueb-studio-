# FACE NOT RECOGNIZED: screenplay

**Twist Villa Short #1** · 57.6 seconds · 9:16 · English · original story
**Logline:** When her phone and her front door stop recognizing her face, Elise catches a glimpse
of an old woman in the mirror, and learns the lenses in her eyes have kept her 37 for thirty years.

**POV rule:** we see Elise the way *she* sees herself (37) — except the mirror flash and the moment we
see her through her son's eyes (67).

**Clues → payoff**
| Clue | Planted | Paid off |

### S01 · HOOK · 0.0–4.5 s · INT. APARTMENT CORRIDOR, NIGHT
Extreme close-up. ELISE (37 as she sees herself: chestnut hair, cream cable-knit turtleneck) raises
her phone to her face. The cold white glow snaps to **pulsing red** across her skin. She frowns and
tries again. A faint electric-blue ring glints at the edge of her iris.
**ON SCREEN (edit):** `✕ Face not recognized` (1.2 s)
**SFX:** two soft error buzzes (1.2 s) · corridor hum
**MUSIC:** M01 enters, low tense pulse
**ELISE (V.O.)** *(0.4 s)*: My phone stopped recognizing my face tonight.

### S02 · 4.5–10.0 s
Side tracking shot. Elise walks to her walnut door and looks into the facial-recognition panel. Its
light ring glows white, then **amber**. She presses the handle. It doesn't move. She tries again.
**ON SCREEN:** `Unknown visitor · Access denied` (6.8 s)
**SFX:** scan chime (6.1 s) · denial tone (6.8 s) · locked handle
**ELISE (V.O.)** *(6.1 s)*: Then my own front door.

### S03 · CLUE · 10.0–15.0 s
Over her shoulder: the amber ring pulses around the lens; her face is reflected in the dark glass.
She leans closer, unblinking.
**ON SCREEN:** `Estimated age: 67` (10.4 s)
**ELISE (V.O.)** *(12.2 s, almost offended)*: I'm thirty-seven.

### S04 · CRACK 1 · 15.0–20.0 s
Macro on her grey-green eye. The thin blue ring around the iris **flickers, stutters, dies**. She
blinks twice and touches the corner of her eye.
**SFX:** electrical crackle (15.6 s)
**ELISE (V.O.)** *(15.8 s)*: Then my eye flickered… like a dying screen.

### S05 · 20.0–24.0 s

**ELISE (V.O.)** *(20.3 s)*: The elevator mirror was always kind to me.

### S06 · MIRROR FLASH · 24.0–25.0 s
**One second.** The mirror shows ELISE AT 67: silver-white hair, the same cream turtleneck, the same
pose, eyes wide.
**SFX:** glass shimmer · the music dips

### S07 · 25.0–29.0 s
Close-up. Young Elise steps back, a hand over her mouth; her gold wedding band catches the light.
**ELISE (V.O.)** *(25.6 s)*: That woman wore my sweater. My ring. My eyes.

### S08 · ARRIVAL · 29.0–35.0 s
Over her shoulder: the elevator doors open, warm light spills out. THEO (42, navy wool coat, her
grey-green eyes) steps out holding a **small white lens case**. He sees her, and his face softens

**SFX:** elevator ding (29.4 s), footsteps · **MUSIC:** M01 fades out by 35.0 s (silence before the twist)

### S09 · TWIST · 35.0–42.5 s
**Reverse, over Theo's shoulder, and for the first time we see Elise as he sees her: 67**, silver
hair, the same sweater, clutching her phone to her chest.
**SFX:** low cinematic sting (35.0 s)
**THEO** *(36.2 s, gently, as if he's said it many times)*: Mum… the lenses died again.

### S10 · REALISATION · 42.5–50.0 s

**MUSIC:** M02 enters, soft piano
**THEO** *(43.5 s)*: Everything's still set to the face you remember.

### S11 · ECHO · 50.0–57.6 s
The opening framing again: Elise's face lit by her phone, **37 again**. The blue ring in her iris

**ON SCREEN:** `✓ Welcome home, Elise` (51.4 s)
**SFX:** unlock chime (51.4 s) · door lock click (52.2 s)
**ELISE (V.O.)** *(50.3 s)*: Thirty years ago, I chose the face I'd keep.
**ELISE (V.O.)** *(54.6 s)*: Tonight, I chose it again.
